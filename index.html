Not a substitute for medical advice

These are traditional home remedies for everyday, mild discomfort — not medical treatment, and not meant to diagnose or cure anything. Every body reacts differently, so stop a remedy if it doesn't suit you.

When to see a doctor instead

If a symptom is severe, lasts more than 2-3 days, keeps returning, or involves fever, difficulty breathing, or intense pain — don't self-treat. For anything urgent or serious, see a general physician right away.

For personalised Ayurvedic guidance

For a proper Ayurvedic diagnosis or a treatment plan suited to you specifically, consult a BAMS-qualified Ayurvedic doctor (Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery) — for example, at a recognised Ayurvedic hospital, wellness centre, or AYUSH clinic near you. This app is a starting reference, not a replacement for their guidance.
